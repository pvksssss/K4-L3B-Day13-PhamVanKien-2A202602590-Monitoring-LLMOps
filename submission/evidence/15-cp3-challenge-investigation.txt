ROOT CAUSE
----------
The released rag_slow incident sets STATE["rag_slow"] in app/incidents.py. While enabled, app/mock_rag.py::retrieve calls time.sleep(2.5). Langfuse independently measured 2500-2501 ms for all five retrieval spans, while generation took about 151-152 ms. This isolates the injected retrieval delay as the source of the latency increase.

FIX AND RECOVERY CHECK
----------------------
Disabled the released incident with `python scripts/inject_incident.py --disable` after the challenge batch. Health then reported rag_slow=false. A post-mitigation request returned HTTP 200 in 152 ms with correlation ID req-e3f8dbd5. Its Langfuse trace ID is 3b13811f7d170b5afae8865a6a5ca0d7; retriever duration was 0 ms and generation duration was 152 ms.

PREVENTIVE MEASURE
------------------
Keep the HighLatencyP95 alert (>3000 ms P95 for 5 minutes) and its Metrics -> Logs -> Traces runbook active. During an incident, inspect TTFT and response latency, find the request by correlation ID, then compare retrieval and generation span durations. For this injected scenario, the immediate mitigation is to disable rag_slow. The challenge threshold of 2000 ms is a diagnostic threshold; it is distinct from the 3000 ms service SLO/alert threshold.
